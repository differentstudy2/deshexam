# **App Name**: DeshExam

## Core Features:

- Mock Test Interface: Distraction-free interface with timer and progress tracker, instant scoring, and detailed breakdown.
- Free Online Quizzes: Wide range of subject-based quizzes, always free to attract new learners, gamified with streaks and badges.
- Practice Questions Display: Organized by class, subject, and chapter. Includes explanations after every question and difficulty filters.
- AI Learning Path Generator: Personalized learning path from test results, highlights weak areas with an AI study plan, and visual improvement charts. Uses an AI tool to choose how best to emphasize a student's particular weak areas.
- Solved Textbook Page Assistant: Upload textbook pages (scan/PDF). AI generates summaries, explanations, solved answers.
- Review Tool: Simple Like/Dislike system. AI ranks most useful content higher.
- Test History & Downloads: Students can review past tests, download PDF/Excel reports, and track progress over time.
- Leaderboard (Gamification): Subject-wise, overall, fastest completion leaderboards. Weekly/monthly resets for fair competition. Rank-up badges.
- Admin/Teacher Dashboard: Create tests (MCQ, True/False, Short, Essay). Upload notes, PDFs, videos. Assign content by class/subject. Track students + bulk reports.
- Content Access Control: Differentiates free content (quizzes, limited tests), paid content (premium mock tests, solved papers), and member-only content (exclusive access to advanced features).
- Subscription System (Recurring Plans): Multiple plans for different user needs including Free, Premium (one-time purchase), and Pro (recurring) plans with varying access levels to features like the AI Learning Path Generator and Solved Textbook Assistant. Family/School plan available. Auto-renew & cancellation options. Free trial for Pro features.

## Style Guidelines:

- Free Content: Green label.
- Paid Content: Gold label.
- Member-only: Purple lock badge.
- Subtle unlock animations when upgrading.
- Clear pricing cards with plan comparison.
